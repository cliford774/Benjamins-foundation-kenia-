About Us

Our Non-Profit Day Care Center in Mombasa

Benjamin’s Foundation Kenia is a non-profit Day Care Center located in Mombasa, East Africa. We nurture and educate more than 30 vulnerable children aged from 3 to 12 years old, providing them with safe early education completely free of charge.

Cultural Immersion & Homestay: You will get to stay directly with the family of our school director, enjoy the warm coastal weather, share local meals, and experience true Kenyan community life. Send us a message for more details!

Airport Pickup Options

We coordinate pickups via Jomo Kenyatta International Airport (NBO) or directly at Moi International Airport (MBA) in Mombasa.

Age & Eligibility

Suitable for individuals aged 18+, but we also warmly welcome complete families with children!

Transparent Expense Note

Zero Program Fee

We do not charge anything to volunteer with us. To cover direct living costs, volunteers simply pay a nominal contribution to cover baseline utilities and local meals.

Living Costs Contribution
€12 / Day
Includes: 3 daily meals, private room stay, WiFi access, safe water, toilet paper, and kitchen cooking gas.

What You Get In Return

  • Private bedroom in a friendly host family setting
  • Discounted and free trips across Kenya visiting National Parks & Safaris
  • Camping trips and organized weekend excursions
  • Authentic African gifts to take back home
